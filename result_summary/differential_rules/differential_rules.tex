\newcommand{\netdef}{An FOV is eligible only when it contains at least 20 cells of
both rule types. Its state is attraction, avoidance, or no rule; net score is
$100(N_{\rm attraction}-N_{\rm avoidance})/N_{\rm eligible}$.}

\newcommand{\summaryvalues}{The four rows use the same rule and clinical stages.
\textbf{How often it fires:} attraction and avoidance are percentages of eligible FOVs;
the numbers below each stage are raw attraction $|$ avoidance $|$ eligible counts, with an
all-zero direction omitted. \textbf{What it is made of:} dots are the two cell-type shares
inside each eligible FOV and diamonds are stage means; this is an abundance control, not a
finding. \textbf{How strong:} dots are Lift in rule-bearing FOVs and diamonds are medians;
Lift 1 is chance. \textbf{Every FOV:} bars include attraction, no rule, avoidance, and
insufficient-cell fields, with net score above. These FOV percentages are descriptive;
fields from one patient are clustered. \netdef}

Rule-bearing examples are nearest their state's median Lift; a no-rule figure uses eligible
comparison fields, preferring fields where the rule could be tested and falling back to
fields with too few patches or expected meetings only when none is left.}

\subsection{Known biological pairs tested in both directions}

Six literature-selected pairs are checked retrospectively; the notebook shows both
directions with the shared rule summary by stage, whole-organ counts and typical tissue
examples.

SMV $\rightarrow$ Endothelial attracts in 26/37 eligible colon fields,
recovering an expected vessel-wall relationship \cite{hellstrom1999}.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{biology_Colon_clinical_SMV_to_Endothelial_summary.pdf}
  \caption{\summaryref{} Both directions use the same FOVs with at least 20 cells of each
  type. Whole-organ counts below pool the stages.
  Patient $q$ values below compare the two arrows by a two-sided sign test after averaging
  FOVs within biopsies and biopsies within patients; FDR uses the notebook's joint
  119-test family.
  The same eligibility filter is applied to the reverse arrow. Here attraction occurs in
  26/37 fields versus 12/37 in reverse; the patient sign test gives FDR $q=0.0021$.}
  \label{fig:biology-guide}
\end{figure}

The highlighted vascular cells show where the recurrent colon rule sits within whole fields.

\begin{figure}[H]

\section{New findings}

Automatic severity and time screens ranked both rule directions. The visually clear
results below are exploratory FOV comparisons, without patient-level significance claims.
The statistical-stream result, Paneth $\rightarrow$ Epithelial Lift, is in Paper alignment.
