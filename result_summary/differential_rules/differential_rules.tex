\newcommand{\netdef}{An FOV is eligible only when it contains at least 20 cells of
both rule types. Its state is attraction, avoidance, or no rule; net score is
$100(N_{\rm attraction}-N_{\rm avoidance})/N_{\rm eligible}$.}

\newcommand{\summaryvalues}{The four rows use the same rule and clinical stages.
\textbf{How often it fires:} attraction and avoidance are percentages of eligible FOVs;
the numbers below each stage are raw attraction $|$ avoidance $|$ eligible counts, with an
all-zero direction omitted. \textbf{What it is made of:} dots are the two cell-type shares
inside each eligible FOV and diamonds are stage means; this is an abundance control, not a
finding. \textbf{How strong:} dots are Lift in rule-bearing FOVs and diamonds are medians;
Lift 1 is chance. \textbf{Every FOV:} bars include attraction, no rule, avoidance, and
insufficient-cell fields, with net score above. \netdef}

\newcommand{\summaryref}{Read as in Figure~\ref{fig:summary-guide}.}

\newcommand{\fovvalues}{Columns are stages or time windows, one rule state per figure. Each
example appears first as a full FOV and then with only the rule's cell types highlighted.
Rule-bearing examples are nearest their state's median Lift; a no-rule figure uses eligible
comparison fields.}

\newcommand{\fovref}{Maps and example selection follow Figure~\ref{fig:fov-guide}.}

\subsection{Known biological pairs tested in both directions}

Six literature-selected pairs are checked retrospectively; the notebook shows both
directions, whole-organ prevalence, stage-specific results and typical tissue examples.

SMV $\rightarrow$ Endothelial occurs in about 70\% of eligible colon fields,
recovering an expected vessel-wall relationship \cite{hellstrom1999}.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{biology_Colon_SMV_to_Endothelial_summary.pdf}
  \caption{Both directions use the same FOVs with at least 20 cells of each type.
  All pools the organ's fields; the other columns split those same fields by clinical stage.
  Top: attraction and avoidance counts divided by eligible FOVs; counts below are
  attract $|$ avoid $|$ eligible, omitting an all-zero state.
  Middle: raw mined Lift in passing, eligible FOVs; dots are FOVs, diamonds medians,
  and Lift 1 is the independence baseline. Missing rules have no plotted Lift.
  Bottom: all FOVs, separated into attraction, avoidance, eligible without a rule,
  and insufficient cells.
  Counts below the top panel are attraction $|$ avoidance $|$ eligible FOVs.
  Here attraction occurs in 26/37 fields versus 12/37 in reverse.}
  \label{fig:biology-guide}
\end{figure}

The highlighted cells place the mined vascular relationship back into the tissue.

\begin{figure}[H]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{biology_Colon_SMV_to_Endothelial_fovs_attraction.pdf}
  \caption{Each column shows a clinical stage, with the full FOV above and the same FOV
  highlighting only the two rule types below. One rule state is shown per figure. Maps are
  illustrative fields; highlighted cells are not individual detected interactions.
  Molecular signaling direction cannot be inferred from these maps.}
  \label{fig:biology-fovs}
\end{figure}
