\textbf{Reading key.} $A\to B$ means a conditional spatial association, not cell motion or causality. Attraction has Lift above 1; avoidance has Lift below 1. The 598,093-cell dataset contains 267 fields of view (FOVs), 73 biopsies, and 71 patients after exclusion. Unless stated otherwise, an eligible FOV contains at least 20 cells of each named type. Stored occurrences pass mining FDR $\leq0.05$; investigation gates are support $\geq0.01$ and confidence $\geq0.5$ for attraction, or expected support $P(A)P(B)\geq0.01$ for avoidance. Rules with the same arrow sides are pooled across mining centers by averaging their metrics within each FOV. Reversing the arrow defines a different rule. Cell-percentage panels check eligibility only.

\textbf{Evidence key.} Counts are rule-bearing/eligible FOVs; patient counts are distinct people, not independent fields. Reported $q$ values are Benjamini--Hochberg-adjusted patient-level stage or Lift tests from the indicated screen. None of the new stage/strength contrasts below reaches $q<0.05$; they are exploratory. The parent-reversal screen is descriptive and has no $q$ value. Mining FDR printed inside some maps is an occurrence filter, not a cohort-level test.

\section{New findings}
All rules here first appeared in automatic, configurable visible-effect screens. The screen required adequate eligible fields and recurring rule-bearing patients, then ranked prevalence, conditional Lift, or measured parent--complex trend gaps. Literature is used only to interpret selected rules. The figures show exploratory effects with their denominators; no new result below has a patient-level $q<0.05$.
